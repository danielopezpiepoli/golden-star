Composed of distinguished conservatory-trained musicians, Golden Star String Quartet merges classical prestige with contemporary refinement. From the sublime architecture of Haydn and Beethoven to intimate Cinematic film scores and bespoke bridal arrangements, we curate sublime sonic atmospheres for concert halls, international festivals, and prestigious private estates across Europe.

Special Production & Silent Cine-Concert

Nosferatu: A Symphony of Horror

Dark atmosphere, suspense, and the hypnotic beauty of classical music unite in this extraordinary live cine-concert (~65 mins). Experience F.W. Murnau’s legendary Expressionist silent film accompanied live by the Golden Star Ensemble (string sextet & piano) led by conductor Katarzyna Marek, performing a specialized interpretation of Johannes Brahms.
