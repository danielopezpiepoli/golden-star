Special Cine-Concert
Cinema in Concert Duration: ~65 mins (String Sextet + Piano)

Nosferatu: A Symphony of Horror (1922)

Live Silent Film Screening with Johannes Brahms Score & Live Conductor

F.W. Murnau's 1922 Expressionist masterpiece performed with live chamber accompaniment (string sextet and piano) under conductor Katarzyna Marek. A dark, hypnotic encounter between German Expressionism and Romanticism.

Cast & Production Highlights

  • Conducted by Katarzyna Marek (Augmented Sextet & Piano)
  • Restored 1922 archival screening synchronized with Brahms repertoire
  • Atmospheric candlelight staging across historic venues

Tribute to Hans Zimmer

Masterworks Series
Chamber Heritage Duration: ~90 mins (With Intermission)

Classical Masterpieces: From Vienna to Paris

Dvořák, Schubert, Ravel & Beethoven

A rigorous celebration of classical chamber music repertoire. Featuring Antonín Dvořák’s String Quartet No. 12 ("American"), Maurice Ravel’s impressionist Quartet in F Major, and select movements from Schubert and Beethoven.

Production Highlights

  • Pure acoustic chamber performance adhering to historical interpretation
  • Ideal for international chamber music festivals and philharmonic seasons
  • Comprehensive program notes provided for venue booklets
